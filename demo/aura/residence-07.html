Proof over promise

What was measured, and how

A claim without its evidence is an adjective. These are the figures behind this page, and where each one came from.

  • 148.60sqm · plan area

    The measured figure, not the marketing figure. The largest plan at AURA Heights measures 217.40 sqm on the same basis, so the two numbers can be compared honestly.

  • 90seconds · to the pier

    Walked and timed at a normal pace, not read off a map. Ninety seconds is the whole distance between the water and the door.

  • 38metres · above the bay

    The height of the headland under the building. The bay lies east, below the pines, which is what puts floor 11 above the morning mist.

  • 6minutes · from Harbor Station

    Station to headland, then the last ninety seconds from the pier on foot. Two numbers, stated separately, because they were counted separately.

  • 06:10the hour it was filmed

    Every one of the 48 residences is filmed in 4K before anyone visits. Residence 07 was filmed at 06:10, golden hour, so the light in the film is light the residence actually gets.

The honest note

Not a bay view, and never sold as one

The bay lies east. Stacks 03 to 05 face the ridge, and a corner stack is never described as a bay view here, whatever the light is doing to the water on the day you visit.
