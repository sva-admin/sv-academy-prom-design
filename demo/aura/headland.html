EAST OVER THE BAY

The headland keeps its own hours

Forty-eight residences stand thirty-eight metres above the water. The bay lies east, so the day arrives over the water and leaves over the ridge, and the building sits between the two like a sundial.

QUIET LANDMARK

One headland, held above the water

This is not a tower on a beach road. The headland rises thirty-eight metres before the building begins, which is high enough that the bay reads as a surface rather than a wall, and low enough that you can still hear it with a window open.

Forty-eight residences share the ground. The largest plan runs to 217.40 sqm, and that figure is measured, not rounded: where a number appears on these pages, someone stood in the room with the instrument that produced it.

NINETY SECONDS, TIMED

The pier is a walk, not a drive

Ninety seconds by the water. We did not estimate that: we walked it, lobby door to first plank, with a stopwatch running, and 90 seconds is what the watch said. No shortcut, no brisk pace, ordinary shoes.

It matters because a pier you can reach in ninety seconds is part of the residence, and a pier you reach by car is a destination. The morning boats, the evening rail, the walk back up with wet hair: all of it happens inside a minute and a half.

SIX MINUTES FROM HARBOR STATION

The city stays exactly six minutes away

Harbor Station is 6 min from the door. Near enough that the city is an errand; far enough that the errand ends. The headland does not pretend to be remote, and it does not pretend to be central. It is six minutes from the platform, and that is the whole claim.

WHAT FACES WHERE

The compass, in plain words

Every view on this site is named for what it is, because a view is the one thing a floor plan cannot show and a sentence can misrepresent. So here is the ground truth we write every page against:

  • EastThe bay. Morning light, open water.
  • Stacks 03 to 05The ridge. These stacks face inland, and we say so.
  • Corner stacksRidge and pine. We never describe a corner stack as a bay view.
